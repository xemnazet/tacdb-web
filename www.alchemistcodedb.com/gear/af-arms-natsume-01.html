    Gear Type Blade

    Basic Information
    Description
    A renowned sword passed down through the Black Star clan, one of Wadatsumi's noble families. Only the leader of the clan may wield it. The clan leader known as the strongest was able to defeat more demons with it than any other, despite owning it for only a short time.
    Attributes
    Attributes: Raises PATK/Slash ATK Power/AGI/Bind Res & Enables Master Ability "'Sword of Black Star' Blue Wave Burial" when equipped on Natsume, Ren, or if job type is Continental Observer (Water Slash ATK on enemy that grows more Powerful as own HP decreases & Absorbs part of Dmg inflicted back as HP [Range: 3, Height Range: 2])
