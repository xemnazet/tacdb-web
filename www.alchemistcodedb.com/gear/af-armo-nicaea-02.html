    Azure Spirit Princess Vest

    Gear Type Armor
    Rarity 5★
    Item Shard
    Memento
    Basic Information
    Description
    A garment worn by a princess to celebrate the New Year alongside her sacred beast companion. It comes with the wishes of the one who gave it to her for good health, and has the power to ward off misfortune. Wearing it brings a moment of peaceful tranquility to this busy princess.
    Attributes
    Raises Max HP, LUCK, Evasion Rate, Jewels Obtained, Strong vs New Year's & Enables Master Ability when equipped on Nicaea or the "Continental Observer" job type
