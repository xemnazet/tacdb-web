    Basic Information
    Description
    A set of armor used by the Captain of the 2nd Regiment, Monzein, when he was just starting out in the Holy Order. It has retained its steely sheen to this day, as the paladin's dedication to chivalry has led him to dutifully polish all of his weapons and armor.
    Attributes
    Attributes: Max HP Up, PDEF Up, Sleep Res Up, Silence Res Up, Enables Master Ability "Sacred Lord" when equipped on Monzein if his job type is "Indomitable Axe General" (Consumes large amount of own HP to use Thunder Slash ATK on enemy units within area around self & immensely raises all of own stats for 5 turns & raises MOVE and JUMP by 2 & "Headsman's Axe of the Thunder God" available after use [Area: Cross (3), Height Range: 2]), Enables "Headsman's Axe of the Thunder God" (Thunder Slash ATK (Strong vs Water) on enemy units in area [Range: 4, Area: Square (3), Height Range: 2]), Enables Master Ability "Sacred Tycoon" (After moving, consumes large amount of own HP to perform Thunder Slash ATK & immensely raises all own stats for five turns & raises MOVE/JUMP by 2 [Range: 4, Height Range: 2]) & "Thunder God's Axe" (Thunder Slash ATK on enemy units within area & Strong vs Water & greatly raises own Slash ATK Power for one turn [Range: 4, Area: Square (3), Height Range: 2]) if job type is "Benevolent Thunderlord"
