    Description
    A wand that is always carried by a young girl with magical powers, who also always carries a doll. It enhances her abilities with magic, which she uses to protect her dear friends and family.

    Attributes: MATK Up, All ATK for Dark Units Up, Poison effect turn up, Sleep Up, Enables Master Ability "Nightmare Echo" when equipped on Mia or Continental Observer (Dark Mag ATK on enemy & absorbs enemy unit's Mag before activation for two turns [Range: 4, Height Range: 2])
