    Deadly Book of Truth

    Gear Type Accessory
    Rarity 5★
    Item Shard
    Memento
    Basic Information
    Description
    A book that is mentioned in the text of the ancient Otherworldly book, Concealed Darkness. It was created to purge the dark princess, and those who read it are said to be bound by the dark curse.
    Attributes
    Attributes: Max HP Up, Modify Strong vs Humans/Initial Jewels Up, Enables Gear Ability "Dark Corpse-Slasher", "Dark Corpse-Slicer", "Deadly Lance", "Deadly Bow", "Screaming Axe", "Dark Corpse-Shredder" or "Screaming Bullet" when equipped on certain units with certain gear (ability varies according to unit)
