    Profile

    Hestia is the head goddess of the Hestia Familia, led here from the Labyrinth City Orario in another world. She holds a deep love for Bell and becomes jealous of other women who come near him. However, she has a kind heart and always treats others fairly. This has earned her the respect of the other gods and her followers alike.
