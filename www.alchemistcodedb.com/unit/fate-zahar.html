    Profile

    This being was summoned through a catalyst, mixed up with the mental energies of Zahar, who left a strong presence of Alchemia in the area where the catalyst was found. The catalyst, a pendant with a red gem from a parallel universe, was excavated from the outskirts of Wratharis. The powers of the catalyst lend him the appearance, as well as the abilities of Archer.
