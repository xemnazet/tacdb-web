    Profile

    Etna is a cute, small demon who lives at the Overlord Castle as a vassal of Overlord Laharl. She respected the last Overlord, but she doesn't even have a shred of loyalty towards his son, Laharl. She's quite known for her snide remarks, and her master, Laharl, is not spared from those.
