    Profile

    This being was summoned through a catalyst, mixed up with the mental energies of Lamia, who left a strong presence of Alchemia in the area where the catalyst was found. The catalyst, a sacred sword with an ancient name from a parallel universe, was excavated from the castle ruins of Wratharis. The powers of the catalyst lend her the appearance, as well as the abilities of Saber.
