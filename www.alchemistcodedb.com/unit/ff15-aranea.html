    Profile

    Aranea is commodore of the airborne division of the Niflheim Empire's army, and is known as the "Mercenary Captain." She wields a magitek lance, which she uses to perform aerial attacks. A big sister-like character, she has the total loyalty of her subordinates. She's a free-spirited and forward-thinking woman, but also has a cute side, and when treated like a lady is easily embarrassed.
