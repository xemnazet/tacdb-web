    Profile

    Pride is a homunculus that was summoned from another world and embodies "pride" - one of the Seven Deadly Sins. Although he may look like a child, he is cold, heartless, and willing to sacrifice his own comrades without a moment's hesitation. His true form is a menacing darkness that is able to change shape and manipulate shadows, and he uses these abilities to mercilessly cut down his enemies.
