    Description
    This rabbit figurine is thought to bring good luck to its owner. Simply placing it nearby will produce this effect, but it is also said that when the moonlight hits it the rabbit will come to life and summon a whole herd of its comrades. Although this is only a rumor, many people believe it to be true.
