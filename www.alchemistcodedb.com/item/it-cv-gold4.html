    Description
    This ingot originates from one of the Alchemy mines that once existed around the world. The value of such ingots corresponds to how deep inside the Alchemy mine they were found. Prismatic ones such as this one are extremely rare, and would have been excavated only occasionally from the innermost depths of the mines.
