    Description
    This belt is made from black-dyed leather, entwined with metal and fitted with a gem in its center that is imbued with magical energy. Its simple appearance belies significant defensive power, and it also provides a small amount of protection against water energy, perhaps because the gem set in it was discovered near a source of water.
