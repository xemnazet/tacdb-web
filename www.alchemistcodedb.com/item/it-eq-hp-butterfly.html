    Description
    This charm is adorned with a red butterfly. It causes magical energy and strength to flow through the bearer. Inside it is a crystal, modeled after a Philosopher's Stone, that glows faintly when detecting water energy, making it possible to anticipate floods and other water-related disasters ahead of time
