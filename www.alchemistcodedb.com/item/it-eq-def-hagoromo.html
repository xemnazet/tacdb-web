    Description
    This robe is said to have been worn by a celestial being spoken of in the legends of Wadatsumi. The floating scarf that accompanies it, known as a "hagoromo," is what really makes the robe stand out. It possesses powers that originate not from Alchemia but from some other mysterious source, and grants the bearer protection against disaster.
