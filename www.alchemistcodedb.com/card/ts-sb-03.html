    Description
    A wave of monsters springs from the gap between dimensions and washes over the world. The Shield Hero rises to stand against it, just as the Fan Hero rises to stand against him.

    "As long as I draw breath, I will be there to challenge you for your life."

    Though she holds no grudge against the Shield Hero or Melromarc, still she continues to fight against them. She fights for the world she believes in and for the sake of her dear comrades. And just like the sash around her waist, her determination holds tight. Even if her battle continues for all eternity, she will never give in.
