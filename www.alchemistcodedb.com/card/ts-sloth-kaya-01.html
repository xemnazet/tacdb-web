    She is, without a doubt, incredible. However, I must say that there's really nothing splendid about investigating ruins when you're in a place like this—but I'd never actually tell her that, of course. Her voice interrupts my thoughts, "Yauras, you're not worrying yourself over needless things, are you? You really shouldn't. It isn't like you at all." Even with her sun visor, her eyes are liable to go bad...but I suppose that classifies as a needless worry, too. Come to think of it, it's even less like me to be relaxing by the sea like this, but here I am. At least I know a bit more about how to enjoy myself than she does. (Yauras' Monologue)
